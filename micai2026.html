What the record shows

Four findings from the record

Historical and 2026 model rankings differ

Historical selection favored DeepAR, and the archived assignment picked it for all 96 state targets. Its historical median (5.7% against 26–31%) was not repeated: in the paper window, editions W03–W18, its recorded median was 27.4%. The historical state DeepAR code scores training fit; a common validation protocol is not verified.

Prophet–Ensemble ordering reversed in the paper window

Excluding DeepAR, Ensemble had lower historical sMAPE than Prophet in 61% of targets. In the paper window, editions W03–W18, Prophet was lower in 60%. These comparisons are post hoc, exploratory and based on overlapping targets.

Revision lowered the replay median

In this seven-week replay (re-selecting with editions W03–W11, scoring W12–W18), revision reduced the median from 32.7% to 28.5%. Simple alternatives had lower medians: Prophet alone 27.6%, a pool without DeepAR 26.4%.

How 2026 is going

In the sixteen-week follow-up, bulletin editions W19–W34, the recorded national Ensemble path has 5.91% sMAPE against 6.24% for the naive. Across the 99 targets, the archived assignment has a higher median than the naive (31.0% vs. 27.0%) and a lower sMAPE in 55 of 99. Prophet and Ensemble beat the naive in 70 and 71 of 99. This motivates reassessing the assignment; revision in this later window remains untested.

How the talk updates the paper's analysis

  1. The talk treats the historical state DeepAR scores as training-fit evidence: the historical code skipped cross-validation for states and scored the full-series fit. The exact published runs are not independently linked.
  2. The spread across states is about 22 times (cumulative 2014–2025), not two orders of magnitude.
  3. There are no recurring February–May peaks; the clearest recurring feature is the turn-of-year dip.
  4. The 56 of 99 switches come from re-selection on editions W03–W11.
  5. The talk adds a 16-week descriptive follow-up and a per-target 52-week naive comparison that the paper does not include.

Reproduce

What can be checked, and what cannot

  • 6,336 of 6,336 public forecasts and 1,584 of 1,584 public observations are reproduced exactly from the sealed bundle and the data snapshot.
  • The follow-up specification was registered internally before any error after edition W18 was computed. These bulletin editions were already published when the specification was written. An internal record is not independent evidence of date.
  • Editions and periods: edition N of the bulletin reports epidemiological period N−1. The naive is the value 52 epidemiological weeks earlier. sMAPE uses the public formula and excludes 0/0 pairs.
  • Hashes identify the copies used. Execution dates are not independently verified; this is a retrospective evaluation of recorded trajectories. Forecast periods are mapped to bulletin editions by rule.
  • Targets overlap: national totals sum the states, while general counts need not equal women plus men. Sex targets use within-year differences of cumulative counts, with negative increments clipped to zero. All six estimators use common effective weeks; a 0/0 pair for any estimator excludes that week from all six sMAPE scores.
